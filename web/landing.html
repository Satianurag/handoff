Who is Handoff for?

Adult children and the people helping them coordinate an aging parent’s care. This evaluation release is for fictional care information: explore the sample family or create a care space with sample records and appointments.

Does everyone need to install an app?

No. Handoff works in a web browser. Family members sign in to the care space you invite them to.

How do medical records get here?

Upload a PDF, JPEG or PNG, or import a reviewed attachment from your care inbox. Keep the original and connect it to a visit. Handoff does not automatically connect to your hospital portal.

What does AI do?

It proposes information and next steps from your documents, with references to the source. You review and correct suggestions before they become work. It does not diagnose, prescribe or decide what treatment is appropriate.

Who can see my parent’s information?

Records start private to their uploader. Choose additional readers for each record, and separately choose who can see care essentials. Review the audience before creating a shared responsibility from private information.

What happens when someone accepts a handover?

They choose the responsibilities they can take. Only the accepted work transfers; the saved receipt records that decision. New information remains visible as a later update.

Can I take my information with me?

Yes. Export the information you can access, including original records. You can revoke future sharing and request deletion in Settings. Copies already downloaded or sent outside Handoff cannot be recalled.

Can I explore before signing up?
